# Interview Notes

## 30-Second Project Pitch

"AI Nexus Consulting is my AI automation platform concept for South African businesses. It shows how I design an AI product end to end: a Next.js front end, AI workflow APIs, Supabase-backed lead and portal foundations, model configuration, usage logging, and security-conscious handling of client data."

## What To Emphasize

- You can build beyond toy prompts: UI, API, data model, portal, and operations.
- You understand responsible AI and POPIA-aware data handling.
- You know how to separate public portfolio code from private credentials and customer data.
- You can talk through architecture tradeoffs: service-role keys server-side, RLS, human review, usage logging.

## How It Connects To FlySafair AI Engineer

- AI workflow thinking: turning vague business needs into usable automations.
- Data discipline: logging, metrics, model settings, and auditability.
- Production awareness: environment variables, private data, RLS, server-only secrets.
- Business empathy: local-market language and practical adoption, not AI hype.
