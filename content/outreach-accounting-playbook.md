# Accounting Firm Outreach Playbook

## Offer

Send me one workflow: a weekly report, client intake process, bookkeeping checklist, SARS follow-up, payroll query flow, or spreadsheet-heavy admin task.

I will map what AI can safely automate, what needs human approval, and what the first pilot would cost.

## Positioning

AI Nexus builds human-in-the-loop AI workflows for South African accounting, bookkeeping, and outsourced finance teams. The pitch is not full autopilot. The pitch is faster admin with review, POPIA-aware data boundaries, and a human owner for every risky action.

## Qualification Questions

- What work repeats every week or month?
- Where does your team copy/paste between email, spreadsheets, accounting tools, and SARS/client portals?
- What cannot be sent without human approval?
- What documents or examples would the AI need to reference?
- How would we measure success: hours saved, faster replies, fewer missed deadlines, cleaner handover, or better client visibility?

## First Pilot Ideas

- Client document intake checklist with reminders
- Monthly management report first draft
- SARS/payroll deadline tracker
- Client query triage and draft response assistant
- Bookkeeping catch-up workflow with missing-item detection
- Audit-ready file checklist and status board
